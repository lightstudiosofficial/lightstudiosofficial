01 / VOICE & PERFORMANCE
HINDI DUBBING

Make every character
sound alive.

We adapt performances for Hindi-speaking audiences while keeping the original scene's intent, emotion and character personality at the centre.

What we take care of

  • Hindi translation and dialogue adaptation for natural, speakable lines.
  • Character-wise casting coordination and voice direction.
  • Recording coordination and performance selection.
  • Dialogue editing, retakes and consistency across scenes.
  • Scene-wise sync checks before final delivery.

Our quality approach

We focus on clear pronunciation, believable emotion, consistent character voices and dialogue that fits the pace of the scene. We review the dub against the picture so lines feel connected to the performance—not simply read aloud.

Typical deliveryEdited Hindi dialogue tracks • Scene-wise audio • Final synchronized dub (as agreed)
02 / LANGUAGE & ACCESSIBILITY
SUBTITLING

Every word,
right on time.

Subtitles that are easy to read, accurately timed and faithful to the meaning and tone of the original dialogue.

What we take care of

  • Dialogue transcription and translation into clear Hindi.
  • Subtitle segmentation and readable line breaks.
  • Accurate in/out timing aligned with dialogue.
  • Consistency in names, terminology and character references.
  • Review for spelling, punctuation and timing errors.

Our quality approach

We balance accuracy with readability. The goal is to preserve meaning without overcrowding the screen, while keeping subtitles synchronized with the spoken dialogue.

Typical deliverySRT subtitle file • Other agreed subtitle formats • Timed Hindi subtitles
03 / CLEANUP & PREPARATION
AUDIO EDITING

Clean takes.
Clear dialogue.

Careful editing that prepares voice recordings for a smooth, professional-sounding final mix.

What we take care of

  • Removing unwanted pauses, clicks and distracting noises where possible.
  • Trimming, arranging and organizing dialogue takes.
  • Choosing and joining the best performance takes.
  • Reducing distracting room noise and harshness where the source allows.
  • Preparing clean, organized tracks for synchronization and mixing.

Our quality approach

We aim for clean, natural dialogue without making voices sound artificial. Results depend on the quality of the original recording, so we assess source audio before confirming the cleanup scope.

Typical deliveryEdited voice tracks • Organized audio stems • Mix-ready dialogue
04 / PICTURE & DIALOGUE SYNC
LIP-SYNC

Dialogue that
fits the frame.

We shape timing and line delivery to sit naturally with on-screen mouth movement, scene rhythm and character action.

What we take care of

  • Checking dialogue timing against the picture.
  • Adjusting line placement and pauses to match scene flow.
  • Flagging lines that need adaptation for better timing.
  • Sync review across scenes and dialogue exchanges.
  • Coordinating timing notes for required retakes.

Our quality approach

We prioritize believable timing and natural delivery. When a literal translation does not fit the mouth movement or available time, we adapt the wording while preserving the scene's intent.

Typical deliverySynchronized dialogue • Timing notes • Scene-wise reviewed audio
05 / ATMOSPHERE & IMPACT
SOUND EFFECTS DESIGN

Let the scene
sound complete.

Thoughtful sound effects that support the action, environment and emotional energy of a scene.

What we take care of

  • Selecting and placing effects to support on-screen action.
  • Building atmosphere with environmental and background effects.
  • Layering impacts, movement and transitions where appropriate.
  • Maintaining perspective and continuity between shots.
  • Preparing effects tracks for the final mix.

Our quality approach

Effects should strengthen the story, not distract from it. We pay attention to timing, level, space and continuity so the soundscape supports dialogue and picture.

Typical deliveryDesigned SFX tracks • Atmosphere layers • Organized stems
06 / FINAL AUDIO
MIXING & MASTERING

One balanced
final sound.

A cohesive final audio pass that brings dialogue, music and effects together with clarity and controlled levels.

What we take care of

  • Balancing dialogue, music and sound effects.
  • Managing levels and dynamics for consistent listening.
  • EQ and processing to improve clarity where needed.
  • Checking transitions, peaks and overall loudness.
  • Exporting agreed formats and reviewing the final render.

Our quality approach

We aim for intelligible dialogue, controlled peaks and a balanced presentation. Export settings and loudness targets are agreed according to the platform or client delivery specification.

Typical deliveryFinal mixed audio • Agreed export format • Stems if included in scope
QUESTIONS · अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

Good to
know.

How long does a dubbing project take?

It depends on the length and the number of characters. Standard delivery is typically 5–7 days, and rush delivery within 48 hours can be arranged. Share your video length and we will confirm a clear timeline.

How is the cost calculated?

Pricing is based mainly on video length in minutes, plus any add-on services such as lip-sync, mixing and mastering, subtitles or SFX design. Use the cost predictor for an instant estimate. The final quote is confirmed after we review your project.

Can I hear samples before I decide?

Yes. Visit the Our Work page to play Hindi dubbing samples. Full reels are available on request.

What will I receive at the end?

Typically edited Hindi dialogue tracks, scene-wise audio and a final synchronized dub, plus SRT subtitle files if subtitling is included. Delivery formats can be agreed with you.

Do you offer only dubbing, or the full audio package?

Both. You can choose a single service or combine Hindi dubbing, subtitling, lip-sync, audio editing, SFX design and mixing and mastering in one project.

How do I start?

Message us on WhatsApp from the Contact page with your project type, duration and timeline. We will reply with next steps.
